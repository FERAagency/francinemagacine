About

The Artist

My name is Francina Pasarelli, author and illustrator from Córdoba, Argentina. I have been working as a freelance illustrator since 2018, specialising in children's and young adult illustration. I work across different formats — from visual development for animated productions (colour keys, backgrounds and dossier layout) to picture books, educational publications and interactive illustrated stories. My approach is technical and commercial, oriented towards the editorial sector.

My main interest lies in working with colour and texture. My illustrations offer a safe space where viewers can relax into the experience — themes of home, nature, light, play and rest run through my work. I am passionate about books and work primarily with publishers, authors and writers. If you are interested in collaborating, feel free to get in touch.
